Rules: differences under 1px and the same colour written differently (#FFF = #FFFFFF = rgb(255, 255, 255)) count as matches. A check is "Could not verify" when one side has no value; it is never guessed and is left out of the score. Score = matched ÷ (matched + different). Major: colour, font family or weight, structure, wording, line count and gaps of 8px or more. Minor: everything else. Confidence High: both values read directly. Med: derived, or matched by similar wording (the row says why).
